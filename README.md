# Padel Ecommerce Framework — €10k Net/Month

A complete, honest, **editable** planning and execution framework for building a padel-focused
ecommerce brand toward a target of **€10,000 NET profit per month**.

> ⚠️ **Honesty first.** This framework does **not** promise a guaranteed winning product — those don't
> exist. Every benchmark is an **assumption to validate** with your own data. The goal is a realistic
> operating model, defensible numbers to test, and a disciplined path that protects your cash through the
> loss-making testing phase. Most stores fail by underestimating CAC, overestimating early profit, and
> running out of money. This framework is built to avoid that.

---

## Why padel?
Chosen deliberately over saturated "trending wellness" categories because, on a low-risk budget, a
**passionate sport niche where you're the customer** means lower ad costs, authentic creative, and a
community to retain. Padel specifically: fastest-growing racket sport, **European home advantage** (~57–58%
of the global market), ~25M+ players, **~13% CAGR**, and a **genuine consumable ladder** (balls & grips wear
out) that turns your Klaviyo retention skill into recurring revenue. Full thesis + sources in
[`business-plan.md`](./business-plan.md).

**Strategy in one line:** enter on **accessories + consumables + bundles** (where brand loyalty is weak and
margins are good), build an audience and an email list, then introduce a **private-label hero racket** once
demand is proven. Lowest-risk path for your capital.

## Recommended order of execution

**Phase 1 — Decide & model (Week 1)**
1. Read [`business-plan.md`](./business-plan.md) — understand the economics and the plan.
2. Open [`financial-model.xlsx`](./financial-model.xlsx) — change inputs (AOV, CAC, retention) and *feel* the
   levers. This is your decision compass.
3. Use [`niche-validation-checklist.md`](./niche-validation-checklist.md) — confirm padel (or score a
   sub-segment / new idea) objectively before committing.

**Phase 2 — Brand, source & build (Weeks 2–4)**
4. [`brand-pack.md`](./brand-pack.md) + [`brand/logos/`](./brand/logos/) — lock the name, logo, palette and
   voice (clear the domain + trademark first). Everything downstream inherits this.
5. [`supplier-sourcing.md`](./supplier-sourcing.md) — line up EU fast-shipping stock + POD; quote a first
   private-label item. **Order samples.**
6. [`shopify-store-buildout.md`](./shopify-store-buildout.md) — build the store: hero offer + starter bundle +
   3–5 accessories/consumables. Don't over-build.

**Phase 3 — Retention before traffic (do NOT skip)**
7. [`klaviyo-retention-plan.md`](./klaviyo-retention-plan.md) — build the day-one flows (welcome, abandoned
   cart, browse abandon, post-purchase, **replenishment**) **before** you run a single ad. This is where the
   business actually becomes profitable.

**Phase 4 — Create, test, learn, scale (Month 1+)**
8. [`content-engine.md`](./content-engine.md) — stand up the AI content pipeline that creates, brand-checks,
   schedules and publishes ad + social creative (with approval gates).
9. [`ad-testing-framework.md`](./ad-testing-framework.md) — spend the test budget with discipline: clear
   hypotheses, fast kills, patient scaling.
10. Feed real numbers (AOV, CVR, CAC, return rate, reorder rate) back into the model and iterate everything.

**Phase 5 — Automate with an AI agent team (run alongside Phases 1–4)**
11. [`ai-agent-team.md`](./ai-agent-team.md) (overview) + [`agents/`](./agents/) (detailed specs) — assign an
   AI agent to each department to *operate* the playbooks above continuously (monitor, draft, analyse, alert).
   **Build the playbooks first**, then automate them. Start with the Phase-1 agents (Finance, Email/Retention,
   Social/Creative), keep human-in-the-loop gates on anything that spends money or signs a supplier, and expand
   only as trust and cash allow. This is augmentation, not autopilot.

*operate* these playbooks continuously — you stay the executive on every decision that spends money or commits the brand.

## The three honest truths to remember
1. **You don't get rich on the first sale.** At mid-ticket AOV with ~40% COGS, first-order ROAS is near
   break-even. **Retention is the profit.** (Hence the detailed Klaviyo plan.)
2. **A low-risk budget means a longer runway to signal.** Expect a product/offer signal around **month 3–4**,
   and a clean €10k net month more likely around **months 8–12** than month 6. Months 1–2 are loss-making by design.
3. **Cash discipline beats optimism.** Ad spend and inventory tie up cash *before* profit lands. Scale from
   results, keep a reserve, and never bet the runway on a hoped-for winner.
