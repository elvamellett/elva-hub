**5. How fast are refunds?**
Once we receive and check your return, we process refunds within **14 days** to your original payment method. Bank processing can add a few days on top.

**6. How do I choose the right racket?**
Start with your level and how you play, not the brand on the frame:
- **New or improving?** Go **round shape**, **lighter weight**, **even or head-light balance**. Forgiving and easy on the arm.
- **All-round / intermediate?** A **teardrop** shape balances power and control.
- **Advanced / aggressive?** A **diamond** shape, head-heavy, rewards good technique with power.
Full breakdown is in the Size & Spec Guide below. Still unsure? Email us your level and playing style and we'll point you to two or three options.

**7. What weight racket should I get?**
As a rough guide: lighter frames (around 350–365g) are easier to swing and kinder on the arm; heavier frames (around 365–385g) add power and stability but ask more of your technique. Comfort first — a racket you can swing relaxed for a full match beats a powerful one that tires you out.

**8. How do I get the grip size right?**
Padel rackets come in one core grip size; you fine-tune the feel with overgrips. If the handle feels too thin, add an overgrip or two. The Size & Spec Guide covers measuring and overgrip stacking.

**9. Indoor or outdoor balls — what's the difference?**
They're close but not identical. Outdoor balls tend to be a touch more durable for rougher surfaces; indoor/all-court balls suit smoother courts and faster play. For most club players, a quality all-court ball is the safe default. Details in the Size & Spec Guide.

**10. What is the Replenish Pack / subscription?**

**11. How do I manage or cancel my Replenish Pack?**

**12. What payment methods do you accept?**

**13. Do you offer exchanges?**

## 9. Size & Spec Guide

### Pick the gear that fits your game.

No single "best" racket — the right one matches your level, body and style. Here's how to choose, in plain language.

**Racket shape**
- **Round** — balance point near the centre/lower frame, larger sweet spot. **Most forgiving and easiest to control.** Best for beginners, improvers, and anyone who values comfort and consistency.
- **Teardrop** — sweet spot in the middle, a blend of power and control. **The versatile all-rounder.** Best for intermediate players who want a bit of everything.
- **Diamond** — balance point higher up the frame, smaller sweet spot. **Most power, least forgiving.** Best for advanced, aggressive players with solid technique.

**Weight (rough guide)**
- **Lighter (~350–365g):** easier to swing, quicker at the net, kinder on the arm. Great for beginners and players who want manoeuvrability.
- **Heavier (~365–385g):** more power and stability through the ball, but more demanding over a long match.
- **Tip:** comfort first. A racket you can swing relaxed for a full match beats a powerful one that wears you out.

**Balance**
- **Head-light / even:** control-focused, easier handling, less strain. Good for beginners and control players.
- **Head-heavy:** more power and momentum on the swing, suits advanced/aggressive play.

**Grip sizing**
- Padel rackets generally come in **one core grip size**; you tune the feel with **overgrips**.
- Too thin? Add **1–2 overgrips** to build it up to a comfortable thickness.
- Quick check: holding the racket, you should have a small gap between fingertips and palm — not cramped, not loose.
- Replace overgrips when they get slick or worn (Replenish can handle this for you).

**Balls: indoor vs outdoor**
- **All-court / indoor balls:** suit smoother surfaces and faster play; consistent bounce indoors.
- **Outdoor balls:** generally a touch more durable for rougher outdoor courts.
- **For most club players:** a quality all-court ball is the safe default.
- Balls lose pressure with use — fresh balls play noticeably better. Keep a spare tube on hand (or on Replenish).

**Apparel & footwear (quick notes)**
- Choose breathable, moisture-wicking layers you can move freely in.
