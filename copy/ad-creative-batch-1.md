# PULSO — Launch Ad Creative, Batch 1 (Meta)

**Brand:** PULSO · padel gear · EU · pulso.ie
**Voice:** knowledgeable, encouraging fellow player. Energy + wellbeing. Honest. No hype, no fake scarcity, no medical/performance claims.
**Taglines:** "Everything but the court." / "Find your pulso."
**Structure:** organized by ANGLE × AUDIENCE. Hooks ≤8 words, headlines ≤5 words.

## RECOMMENDED FIRST TEST PLAN (€500–1,000/mo)
Mapped to ../ad-testing-framework.md: broad audiences (let Meta find buyers), creative ≈ 80% of performance, **test hooks first**, kill losers fast. At this budget, run **one lean campaign, broad targeting, few ad sets, many creatives** — don't fragment spend across micro-audiences.

**Structure:** 1 campaign (sales/purchase) → 1–2 broad ad sets (EU, age 18+, minimal targeting). Concentrate budget so each creative can clear the learning threshold.

**Launch these 5 creatives first** (one per priority hook/angle, mixing video + static):

| # | Angle | Asset | Why first |
|---|-------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | A — Problem/Solution | A-UGC-1 "Dead ball test" | Sharpest, most universal pain; UGC hook in 3s. Likely top performer. |
| 2 | B — Beginner-enabler | B-UGC-1 "First-timer unbox" | Starter Kit is the hero AOV-builder (€99.99); taps growing-sport demand. |
| 3 | A — Problem/Solution | A-S2 grip macro (static) | Cheap to produce; tests grip hook as a static control vs. video. |
| 4 | C — Community/identity | C-UGC-1 "Tuesday four" | Brand-building, broad-resonance angle; tests identity vs. pain hooks. |
| 5 | D — Gifting | D-S1 giftable bundle (static) | Low-cost static; keeps a gifting line warm year-round, scales seasonally. |

**Method:**
- **Hooks first:** the first 3s carries each test. If a video underperforms, swap the hook before killing the whole concept.
- **Budget pacing:** ~€20–33/day total. Give each creative a few days; don't judge on day-one noise.
- **Kill criteria:** cut creatives that can't trend toward break-even **first-order ROAS ≈ 1.77** (CAC ≈ €37 at €70 AOV). Watch hook-rate (3s views) and CTR as early signals before CAC stabilizes.
- **Blended target:** steer toward **MER ≈ 2.3–2.5** overall, not per-ad perfection.
- **Iterate winners:** when one angle clears bar, produce 2–3 hook variants of it next batch before broadening angles.
- Rotate in remaining hooks/angles from this doc as the kill/scale cycle frees budget.

## COMPLIANCE NOTE
- **No medical or performance claims.** Avoid "improves your game," "boosts performance," "prevents injury," "better grip = better health." Keep to honest, functional benefits (balls hold bounce; overgrips reduce slipping; kit gets you court-ready).
- **No fake scarcity / no false urgency.** No invented countdowns or "only X left" unless literally true.
- **Disclose paid UGC.** Creator/partnership content must carry clear paid-partnership disclosure (Meta Branded Content tag + visible "#ad"/"paid partnership"). Creator opinions stay genuine.
- **Secure creator rights.** Signed agreement covering usage rights, whitelisting/Spark Ads permissions, term, territory (EU), and music/likeness clearances before running any creator asset as a paid ad.
- **Pricing/claims accuracy.** Prices in ads must match live pulso.ie listings; update creative if prices change.
