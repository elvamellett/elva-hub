## 3. Repurposing + Scheduling

**Repurpose the winners.**
- Track each post for 5-7 days. Anything beating your median saves/shares is a **winner** — don't let it die in the feed.
- Hand winning organic Reels to **Paid Ads** as proven creative (organic engagement is your cheapest pre-test). Flag the top 2-3 each fortnight for the ads team.
- Re-cut long Reels into 9s Stories; turn carousels into single-image posts; pull caption hooks into email subject lines and the link-in-bio.
- Reshare strong UGC and "Add Yours" responses into Stories and Highlights to keep community pillars fed without new production.

**Schedule, don't post live.**
- Batch a week at a time and queue via **Buffer**, **Metricool**, or **Meta Business Suite** (native scheduling also covers IG + FB; use a 3rd-party tool for TikTok + analytics in one view).
- Keep Stories and reactive/community posts manual — they perform better when they feel live.

**Best-time posting (EU audience, starting point — let your own analytics override).**
- **Weekdays:** ~12:00-13:00 (lunch) and ~18:00-21:00 (after work / after play).
- **Weekends:** late morning ~10:00-12:00, when people are heading to or back from court.
- Reels/TikTok skew slightly later in the evening; check your Metricool/Insights heatmap after 2 weeks and lock your top 3 slots.
