# PULSO — Email Flow Copy (Klaviyo)

*Everything but the court. Find your pulso.*

> **All copy below is editable** — swap product names, prices, delays, and offers to match your live catalogue and margins. Treat subject lines and incentives as A/B starting points, not gospel.
>
> **Deliverability reminder:** Authenticate your sending domain (DKIM/SPF/DMARC) before any of this goes out. Send to **engaged segments first** (30/60/90-day openers/clickers) to build and protect sender reputation, then expand. Every send needs a clear unsubscribe and a physical postal address in the footer. Only mail contacts with **explicit GDPR opt-in**; SMS consent is separate. No fake scarcity, no medical or performance claims — keep urgency honest (real offer end dates, real stock).
