# PULSO — Supplier Outreach Kit

Ready-to-send email templates for sourcing padel gear (pulso.ie). Pairs with the
sourcing strategy and vetting question bank in `./supplier-sourcing.md`.

---

## How to use this kit

1. **Pick the template** that matches the route (EU distributor, China agent, sample, follow-up, negotiation).
2. **Fill the placeholders:** `[SUPPLIER NAME]`, `[YOUR NAME]`, `[CONTACT DETAILS]`, `[PRODUCT]`, `[QTY]`, etc.
3. **Cut anything you don't need.** Shorter beats thorough on a first contact — you're opening a conversation, not closing a deal.
4. **Sequence it to the strategy:** EU distributors + POD first (launch), then a small private-label order via a China agent for overgrips/bags, then scale. Don't ask a factory for a 500-unit racket run on email one.
5. **Always order samples before any bulk order.** Non-negotiable.
6. **Log every reply** (price, MOQ, lead time, contact) back into `./supplier-sourcing.md`.

**Tone rules:** credible, specific, concise. You're a buyer with a plan, not a beginner. Never sound desperate, never over-explain, never accept the first quote as final.

## How to evaluate replies

A strong supplier reply usually shows:

- **Samples offered freely** — sends them, states cost/timing without resistance.
- **A clear volume price curve** — real numbers at MOQ 50/100/250/500, not one vague figure.
- **Specific specs** — ball pressure/felt, grip thickness/tack, bag material; not "high quality, best price."
- **Compliance on hand** — CE/EN docs and test reports produced without fuss where relevant.
- **Honest lead times** — production + freight stated separately; EU warehousing/3PL discussed concretely.
- **Defined QC** — names a process and a defect/QC rate.
- **Named contact + response expectation** — a person, time zone, and turnaround.
- **References** — willing to name other brands they supply or show history.
- **Sensible MOQ** — lets you start small / mix SKUs to validate before scaling.
- **Escrow-friendly** — comfortable with Trade Assurance / staged payment on early orders.

## Red flags (walk away or proceed with caution)

- **Refuses samples** or pressures you to skip sampling.
- **No verifiable history or references**; brand-new entity only.
- **Prices too good to be true** — often QC or counterfeit risk.
- **Evasive** on compliance, IP, lead times, or who handles customs/duties/VAT.
- **Pushes a huge first MOQ** before you've validated demand.
- **Slow or poor communication during courtship** — it only gets worse after you've paid.
- **Vague on IP/patents**, especially on racket tech.
