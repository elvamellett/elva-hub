01

What is being built

  • Photograph fills the block, shown uncropped at 3:2 — block height derives from image width, not a fixed value.
  • No overall scrim. A single ombre rises from the bottom edge and dies out at 34% of block height — roughly the middle of the beach huts.
  • Header sits transparently on the photograph, top-aligned.
  • All copy sits at the bottom on the ombre: eyebrow and headline left, caption and one link right.
  • No buttons, no fact strip, no statistics, no scroll cue.

Height is the one thing not to get wrong. height:min(100vw / 1.5, 960px) keeps the photograph uncropped at its native 3:2 up to 1440px wide, then caps. Do not substitute 100vh — the design was approved showing the full building and the tops of the beach huts, and a viewport-height hero crops both.
04

Responsive

WidthBehaviour
≥1100pxAs specified. Two-column body, 340px right column.
700–1099pxRight column drops under the lead: grid-template-columns:1fr, gap:28px, caption max-width:46ch.
<700pxNav collapses to the site's existing mobile menu trigger — reuse it, don't build a second. Body padding 20px. Ombre height 46%. Headline min 34px.

Suppress the headline break below 700px: .hero__title br{ display:none }.

05

Behaviour

  • Header. Static and transparent. The site's existing fixed .bar fades in once the hero has scrolled ~70% out of view, as it does now; just change the trigger element to .hero.
  • Parallax. Keep it, low amplitude only — translate .hero__img by no more than 6% of block height across the hero's scroll. Drop it on mobile if it costs frames.
  • Entrance. Eyebrow, headline, caption and link fade up 8px, 320ms ease-out, staggered 60ms, on load. Nothing else animates.
  • Reduced motion. prefers-reduced-motion: reduce disables parallax and entrance — render the final state immediately.

08

Acceptance

  • Full building and the tops of the beach huts visible at 1440px — no vertical crop.
  • Block height caps at 960px and never uses vh.
  • Ombre reaches zero opacity by 66% up the block; no scrim over sky or façade.
  • Eyebrow renders 12.5px / 500 / chalk, not champagne.
  • Headline holds two lines at ≥1100px.
  • Nav "Home" carries the gold underline via aria-current, not a class.
  • Keyboard tab reaches logo → four nav links → hero link, with a visible gold focus ring.
  • prefers-reduced-motion kills parallax and entrance.
  • Lighthouse: hero image is the LCP element and is preloaded.
