# Brand Pack — Padel Ecommerce

A complete starting brand identity: name options, a recommended direction, logos (real SVG files in
[`brand/logos/`](./brand/logos/)), colour, typography, voice, and imagery guidelines. Built to be **edited** —
swap the name, tweak the palette, and the rest of the system (agents, content engine, store) follows.

> ⚠️ **Validate before you commit.** Domain availability and **trademark clearance** for any name below are
> assumptions you MUST check (EUIPO/your national registry + a `.com`/local-TLD search) before buying or
> building. Names are suggestions, not cleared marks.

---

## 1. Naming — options & recommendation

The strategy is an **improver/community padel gear brand** entering on accessories + consumables + bundles.
A good name should feel **authentic to real players** (insider credibility lowers CAC) yet be easy to say
across the EU and brandable.

### Shortlist (with rationale)

| Name | Style | Meaning / why it works | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VOLEA** ⭐ | Authentic ES | "Volley" in Spanish — short, punchy, premium, easy across EU; brandable | Common word — trademark a stylised mark, not the dictionary word |
| **BANDEJA** | Insider ES | A signature padel shot — instant credibility with real players | Less obvious to total beginners |
| **RALLY** | Accessible | Universal, energetic, community feel; works for all levels | Generic; harder to trademark broadly |
| **VÍBORA** | Edgy ES | The aggressive smash ("viper") — bold, memorable, cool | Accent mark; "viper" connotation may feel aggressive |
| **CRISTAL** | Premium ES | The glass court walls — sleek, upmarket | Used in other categories (beer/champagne) — clearance risk |
| **PISTA** | Minimal ES | "The court" — clean, ownable | Quiet/abstract on its own |
| **DEUCE** | Familiar | Racket-sport scoring term — premium, recognisable | Tennis-leaning; check existing use |
| **VOLTA** | Coined | "Lap/turn"; modern, brandable, energy ("volt") link to the ball | Less padel-specific |

### Recommendation
1. **VOLEA** (primary) — the best balance of authentic, premium, brandable, and EU-friendly. The "O" doubles
   as a padel ball in the logo (see SVGs).
2. **BANDEJA** (alternative) — if you want maximum insider/player credibility.
3. **RALLY** (alternative) — if you want the most beginner-accessible, community-first feel.

> The rest of this pack and the agent/content system are written around **VOLEA**, but every reference is
> swappable — change the name token and the palette/voice carry over.

### Tagline options (VOLEA)
- **"Everything but the court."** — clever positioning for a gear/accessory brand (you sell it all except the court). ★
- **"Level up your game."** — improver focus.
- **"Made for the rally."** / **"Own the rally."** — energetic, community.
- **"Play more padel."** — simple, inviting.

## 2. Logos

Real, editable concept files live in **[`brand/logos/`](./brand/logos/)** — open them in a browser or a vector
editor (Figma/Illustrator/Inkscape):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `volea-primary.svg` | Primary horizontal lockup: padel-ball mark + **VOLEA** wordmark (navy on light) |
| `volea-monogram.svg` | "V" monogram in a rounded tile — for avatars, app icons, favicons |
| `volea-stacked-dark.svg` | Stacked lockup on the dark/navy background (volt accent) |
| `bandeja-wordmark.svg` | Alternative-name wordmark, so you can see a second direction |

> These are **concept SVGs** using a system sans fallback. For final production, set the brand font
> (below) and **convert text to outlines** so the logo renders identically everywhere. Treat them as a
> strong starting point for a designer or a vector tool, not press-ready final art.

**Logo usage basics:** keep clear space around the mark (≈ the height of the "ball"); don't stretch, recolour
outside the palette, add shadows, or place the navy wordmark on a busy/low-contrast background (use the dark
lockup instead).

## 3. Colour palette

**Primary palette — "Court Night"** (premium + the energy of a padel ball)

| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | **Ink Navy** | `#0E1B2A` | Text, backgrounds, wordmark |
| Accent | **Volt** | `#C2F03C` | The ball; CTAs, highlights, energy (use sparingly) |
| Light base | **Chalk** | `#F6F7F2` | Page background, negative space |
| Neutral | **Slate** | `#8A94A3` | Secondary text, borders |
| Deep accent | **Court Teal** | `#0B6E6E` | Secondary brand colour, links |

**Alternative palette — "Clay Court"** (warmer, more lifestyle)
- Ink Navy `#0E1B2A` · **Clay/Coral** `#E8643C` (accent) · Chalk `#F6F7F2` · Sand `#E7DFD2` · Court Teal `#0B6E6E`.

**Contrast/accessibility:** keep body text Ink Navy on Chalk (high contrast). Volt is an **accent only** — never
body text (poor contrast). Ensure buttons meet WCAG AA.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / headings** | **Space Grotesk** (or Clash Display) | Geometric, sporty, modern; free (Google/Fontshare) |
| **Body / UI** | **Inter** | Highly legible, free, pairs cleanly with Space Grotesk |
| Fallback stack | `Inter, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` | For email/web safety |

**Hierarchy:** big bold display headings (tight letter-spacing), generous line-height on body, ALL-CAPS only
for short labels/buttons. Keep it clean and confident — premium sport, not loud dropshipper.

## 5. Brand voice & personality

**Personality:** a knowledgeable, encouraging **fellow player** — the friend at the club who actually knows
the gear and wants you to improve. Confident, warm, honest. **Not** hypey, not "BUY NOW!!!" dropshipper energy.

**Voice principles**
- **Player-to-player, not brand-to-consumer.** Speak from real padel knowledge.
- **Encouraging & inclusive.** Beginners welcome; improvers respected; no gatekeeping.
- **Clear over clever.** Helpful first; wit second.
- **Honest.** No fake scarcity, no unverifiable claims. Trust is the moat.
- **Community-led.** Celebrate the sport, the rallies, the clubs.

**Do say:** "Fresh balls = better bounce = sharper games." · "New to padel? Start here." · "Built for the rally."
**Don't say:** "🔥 INSANE DEAL 🔥", medical/performance claims, anything you can't back up.

**Mini messaging matrix**
| Audience | Hook angle |
|---|---|
| Beginner | "Everything you need to start — in one kit." |
| Improver | "The gear that helps you level up." |
| Regular/competitive | "Match-ready essentials, restocked on your schedule." |
| Gifter | "The padel obsessive in your life will love this." |

## 6. Imagery & art direction
- **Real over staged:** authentic on-court action, sweat, glass walls, the volt-yellow ball mid-flight. UGC > stock.
- **Light & energetic:** natural light, motion, community moments; Chalk/airy backgrounds for product.
- **Product:** clean, well-lit, spec-clear shots on Chalk or Ink backgrounds; ball-yellow as the recurring accent.
- **Consistency:** the **Volt ball** is the recurring visual motif across logo, ads, and packaging.
- **Avoid:** generic gym stock, cluttered collages, low-res supplier images, over-filtered looks.
