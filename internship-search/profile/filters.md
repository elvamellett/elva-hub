# Filters & Priorities

> Applied mechanically every week. **Hard filters drop a role outright.**
> **Priorities rank** survivors — they never drop a role on their own.

## HARD FILTERS — fail any one and the role is dropped

1. **Start date** must be one of:
   - September 2026
   - October 2026
   - November 2026
   - December 2026
   - January 2027
   - February 2027
   - → Anything starting outside this window is dropped.
2. **Duration:** **6 months minimum.** Shorter → dropped.
3. **Type:** **internship or placement only.** Graduate schemes, grad jobs, permanent
   roles, and full-time non-placement positions → dropped.
4. **Language:** **English only.** Any role requiring French, Dutch, or any language other
   than English → dropped. (Irish is fine but rarely required.)
5. **No summer internships.** Summer-dated or summer-branded programmes → dropped.

> If a listing is ambiguous on any hard filter, note it in the roundup as
> **"needs verification"** rather than silently passing or dropping it.

## LOCATION PRIORITY (rank, don't drop)

1. **Dublin / DART-commutable** — Elva lives in Greystones, so anything on the DART line
   counts as commutable. Highest priority.
2. **Manchester** — family there, no rent.
3. **Brighton** — family there, no rent.
4. **London** — **only if exceptional**: a luxury brand or big tech. Otherwise deprioritise.

> Remote / hybrid roles based in any of the above are welcome and rank with their base city.

## SECTOR / INDUSTRY FIT (rank, don't drop)

**Functions wanted:** eCommerce ops, CRM / email marketing, retail ops, digital / eRetail.

**Target industries (rough priority by fit):**
- **Luxury retail:** LVMH houses, Chanel, Dior, Tiffany, Longchamp.
- **Cosmetics / beauty:** Sephora, Estée Lauder, L'Oréal, Sculpted by Aimee,
  The Smooth Company.
- **Jewellery & premium retail.**
- **Big tech with Dublin offices:** Google, Meta, TikTok, Amazon, ServiceNow,
  Colgate-Palmolive.
- **FMCG:** Diageo, PepsiCo, Kraft Heinz.

**Explicitly NOT interested in:** finance, engineering, hospitality.
→ Roles whose core function is finance, engineering, or hospitality are deprioritised hard
  (effectively dropped unless the actual job is CRM/eCommerce/retail-ops within them).
