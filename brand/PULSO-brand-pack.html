PULSO Brand Guidelines · v1.0

The pulse of the game.

PULSO is the Spanish word for pulse — the rhythm of a rally, a body in motion, a community that plays. This guide sets out how the identity looks, sounds and behaves so it stays sharp everywhere it shows up.

01 — The idea

The meaning lives inside the name. The O becomes a pulse reading — a single clean heartbeat traced through the wordmark.

No ball. No racket. Just energy and rhythm, drawn with restraint. A heavy italic wordmark gives it forward motion and a premium, sporting confidence; deep navy keeps it trusted and grown-up; one electric accent supplies the charge.

02 — Primary logo

The horizontal wordmark is the lead asset. Use it wherever space allows. The pulse-disc replaces the O and should never be detached or recoloured on its own.

07 — Clear space & size

Give the logo room. Keep clear space on all sides equal to the height of the pulse mark. Never reproduce it below the minimum sizes.

Horizontal logo — min 120px / 32mm wide

08 — Do & don't

A few rules keep the logo ownable and consistent.

✓Do
  • Keep the wordmark and pulse-disc locked together as supplied.
  • Use navy, white-on-navy, or the blue accent — always high contrast.
  • Give it full clear space and respect the minimum sizes.
  • Use the square mark for app icons and avatars.
✕Don't
  • Never place a URL, “.ie” or tagline inside the logo.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the wordmark.
  • Don't recolour the disc or detach the pulse line from it.
  • Never add a ball, racket or any sports-equipment motif.
