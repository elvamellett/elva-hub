# PULSO — Brand & Logo Design Brief (handoff)

Hand this to **Claude (design)** or any AI design / brand tool to create the visual identity. The block under
**"PROMPT TO PASTE"** is written to be copied directly. Everything above it is context for you.

> Why this exists: the interim brand pack used a literal padel-ball motif the owner didn't like. This brief
> hands logo + visual-identity creation to a design tool, **keeps navy**, and **removes the ball**. The written
> strategy, voice, and all the build assets in this repo stay as-is and will adopt the new identity once it's set.

---

## Non-negotiables (must respect)
- **Brand name:** **PULSO** (Spanish for *pulse / heartbeat*; a wellness/fitness + energy/rhythm meaning).
- **Keep navy** as the primary colour — Ink Navy `#0E1B2A` (or a very close deep navy). Navy = premium, trusted, sporty.
- **No tennis/padel ball** anywhere in the logo. No literal racket either. Avoid sport-equipment clichés.
- **Wordmark-led.** "PULSO" should be legible and ownable. An abstract mark/monogram is welcome but must not be a ball.
- **Premium & modern**, not "dropshipper." Think considered DTC sport/wellness brand, not a clip-art store.
- Must work in **one colour**, **inverted on navy**, tiny (favicon/app icon), and large (hero/packaging).

## Brand context (for the designer)
- **What:** a padel (racket sport) gear ecommerce brand — accessories, consumables (balls, grips), bundles, then private-label. EU market.
- **Audience:** padel players from beginners to improvers to regulars; design-conscious, community-minded, value authenticity over hype.
- **Positioning:** "Improver/community padel gear." Taglines in use: **"Everything but the court."** (positioning) and **"Find your pulso."** (brand line).
- **Personality:** knowledgeable, encouraging fellow player; energy + wellbeing; honest; warm; confident. Not loud or gimmicky.
- **Meaning to lean into:** *pulse / rhythm / heartbeat / energy / flow* — the feeling of moving, playing, being in your pulse.

## Creative directions to explore (pick/blend — designer's call)
1. **Pulse/heartbeat line** abstracted into the wordmark or as a mark (e.g., an ECG/waveform tick) — draws the name's meaning without a ball. *(Strong direction.)*
2. **Rhythm/motion** abstraction — repeating strokes, a beat, a soundwave-like cadence.
3. **Monogram "P"** built from clean geometry or a pulse stroke (for app icon/avatar).
4. **Court geometry** abstraction — subtle lines/angles from a padel court (used restrained, never literal).
5. **Type-only** — a confident custom-feel wordmark with one small distinctive detail.

## Colour
- **Primary:** Ink Navy `#0E1B2A` (keep). Light base: an off-white/chalk for backgrounds.
- **Accent:** propose **one** accent that reads premium + energetic with navy. Open to your recommendation —
  options to consider: a refined electric blue/teal, a warm coral, a metallic/champagne, or a muted lime
  (avoid the bright "tennis-ball" yellow-green that ties back to the ball). Show 1–2 accent options.
- Provide HEX values and a small palette (primary, accent, light, neutral, 1 secondary).

## Typography
- Suggest a **display** face (modern geometric/grotesque) + a clean **body** face (e.g., Inter). Free/Google or
  licensable. Show the wordmark set in the display face.

## Deliverables requested
1. **Primary logo lockup** (horizontal) + **stacked** version.
2. **Logo mark / monogram** (square, for app icon/avatar/favicon).
3. **One-colour** and **inverted-on-navy** versions.
4. **Colour palette** (HEX) with usage notes + **type system**.
5. **Mini usage guide:** clear space, min size, do/don'ts (incl. "never put a URL/.ie inside the logo").
6. (Nice to have) a quick mock on a product/social tile to show it in context.

## Hard don'ts
- ❌ No ball, racket, or literal sports equipment in the mark.
- ❌ No `.ie` / no URL inside the logo.
- ❌ No generic gradients-everywhere, no stocky mascot, no clip-art.
- ❌ Don't abandon navy.
