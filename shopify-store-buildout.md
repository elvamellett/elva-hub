# Shopify Store Buildout Checklist (Padel, Dropship/Hybrid)

A step-by-step setup checklist tuned for a padel store running the **accessory/consumable-led → private-label**
model. Order matters: build the **foundation and Klaviyo flows before you spend a euro on ads.**

> You have strong Shopify ops experience — this is a padel-specific checklist and CRO reminder list, not a
> beginner tutorial. Tick boxes; skip what you already do by reflex.

---

## Phase 0 — Brand & foundations (before the store)
- [ ] **Brand name + domain** — memorable, padel-relevant, EU-friendly; secure `.com` + local TLD if relevant.
- [ ] **Logo + colour palette + 2 fonts** — sporty, premium-but-approachable (you're a brand, not a generic dropshipper).
- [ ] **Positioning one-liner** — who it's for + the wedge (e.g. "Padel gear & essentials for improving players").
- [ ] **Business email** on your domain (e.g. hello@yourbrand.com) — needed for deliverability & trust.
- [ ] Decide initial **catalogue**: 1 hero offer + 1 starter bundle + 3–5 accessories/consumables (don't over-build).

---

## Phase 1 — Core Shopify setup
- [ ] Shopify plan (Basic is fine to start). Clean, fast **theme** (Dawn or a light premium theme).
- [ ] Currency **EUR**; set correct **business address, timezone, units**.
- [ ] **Markets**: enable the EU countries you'll ship to; set per-market currency/language if relevant.
- [ ] **Taxes**: configure EU VAT correctly (register/OSS as required — get accountant advice; this is not optional).
- [ ] Mobile check: **>70% of traffic will be mobile** — test every page on a phone.

---

## Phase 2 — Payments & checkout
- [ ] **Shopify Payments / Stripe** live (cards). Confirm ~2.5% + €0.30 fee assumption against your real rate.
- [ ] **PayPal** (high EU trust), **Apple Pay / Google Pay** (mobile conversion).
- [ ] Local EU methods where relevant: **iDEAL** (NL), **Bancontact** (BE), **Klarna** (DACH/Nordics), **SOFORT**.
- [ ] **Shopify one-page/accelerated checkout** enabled; guest checkout ON.
- [ ] Test a **real end-to-end purchase** (and a refund) before launch.

---

## Phase 3 — Shipping rules
- [ ] **Free shipping threshold** set *above* AOV to push basket size (e.g. free over €60–75 → nudges add-ons).
- [ ] Flat/real rates below threshold; don't eat shipping silently into a thin margin.
- [ ] **Delivery time promise** that matches reality (EU stock: 2–7 days). Under-promise, over-deliver.
- [ ] Hide/avoid slow long-haul methods on consumables (fast delivery is a trust driver in sport gear).
- [ ] Returns: clear policy, EU **14-day right of withdrawal** compliant; decide who pays return shipping.
- [ ] Packaging plan (branded insert/thank-you card — cheap retention + review driver).

---

## Phase 4 — Essential apps (add only what pays for itself)
| Need | App type / example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Email/SMS & flows** | **Klaviyo** | Your edge — install first; see `klaviyo-retention-plan.md` |
| Reviews / UGC | Judge.me, Loox, Okendo | Social proof; Loox = photo reviews (great for sport) |
| Bundles / upsell | Fast Bundle, Rebuy, Shopify Bundles | Build the hero+add-on ladder; raise AOV |
| Replenishment / subscribe | Recharge, Appstle, Shopify Subscriptions | Balls/grips reorders → recurring revenue |
| Cart upsell / cross-sell | Rebuy, ReConvert (post-purchase) | "Add balls/grip?" at cart & after checkout |
| Sticky add-to-cart / CRO | Theme or app | Mobile conversion |
| Wishlist (optional) | — | Browse intent capture |

> Keep the app stack lean at launch. Every app is monthly cost + page-speed drag. Add when justified by data.

---

## Phase 5 — Trust & conversion essentials (the CRO checklist)
Padel buyers are gear-savvy; thin/generic stores lose them instantly.

**Product pages**
- [ ] High-quality images **+ video** (gameplay/use), multiple angles, scale reference.
- [ ] **Specs that matter**: racket shape (round/teardrop/diamond), weight, balance, core (EVA), surface (carbon/fibreglass),
      ball pressure/type, grip thickness — players filter on these.
- [ ] **Benefit-led copy** (control vs power, beginner vs advanced) — not just a spec dump.
- [ ] Clear **bundle/upsell** block ("Complete your kit: balls + grip + bag").
- [ ] Reviews/photos visible above the fold area; star rating near title.
- [ ] **Trust badges**: secure checkout, fast EU shipping, easy returns, real contact.
- [ ] Sizing guide for shoes/apparel (reduces returns).

**Site-wide**
- [ ] **About page** with a real story/face (community credibility — you're a player; use that).
- [ ] **Contact page** + responsive support (email/chat); visible response-time promise.
- [ ] FAQ (shipping, returns, sizing, racket selection help).
- [ ] Announcement bar (free-shipping threshold, current offer).
- [ ] Fast load (compress images, limit apps) — speed = conversion + lower CPA.
- [ ] **Email capture** (popup → Klaviyo welcome flow) live before any traffic.

**Legal / policy pages (required)**
- [ ] Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund/Returns Policy, Shipping Policy.
- [ ] **GDPR** cookie consent banner (EU requirement).
- [ ] Imprint/legal notice where required (e.g. **Impressum** in DACH).
- [ ] Accurate company details (builds trust + legal compliance).

---

## Phase 6 — Padel-specific conversion boosters
- [ ] **"Find your racket" quiz/guide** (beginner / improver / advanced → recommends racket + bundle). Captures email,
      reduces choice paralysis, raises AOV.
- [ ] **Starter bundle** featured prominently (racket + balls + grip + bag at €90–110) — your AOV hero offer.
- [ ] **Replenishment messaging** on consumables ("Most players replace balls every ~3–4 sessions").
- [ ] Collections by **level** and by **use case** (control/power, indoor/outdoor balls).
- [ ] Community hook: link to your IG/TikTok, UGC gallery, club/ambassador angle.

---

## Phase 7 — Pre-launch QA (do not skip)
- [ ] Test purchase on **mobile + desktop**, each payment method, plus a refund.
- [ ] Every Klaviyo flow fires correctly (place a real test order; check welcome/abandon/post-purchase).
- [ ] All policy/legal pages present and linked in footer.
- [ ] Pixel/analytics installed: **Meta Pixel + Conversions API**, GA4, Shopify analytics.
- [ ] Inventory/stock synced with supplier; no overselling.
- [ ] Page speed checked; broken-link/typo pass; 404 page set.
- [ ] Order-confirmation & shipping notification emails branded and accurate.

---

## Phase 8 — Post-launch (first 30 days)
- [ ] Watch **CVR, AOV, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion** daily; fix the biggest leak first.
- [ ] Harvest **reviews + photo UGC** from first buyers (ask in post-purchase flow).
- [ ] A/B the **bundle offer, free-ship threshold, hero image**.
- [ ] Confirm fulfilment SLA is being met; resolve any supplier delays fast (early reviews are fragile).
- [ ] Feed learnings back into `financial-model.xlsx` (real AOV, CVR, return rate, CAC).

> The store exists to make the financial model real: every CRO win raises CVR/AOV, which lowers the revenue
> you need to hit €10k net. Build it well, then let Klaviyo do the compounding.
