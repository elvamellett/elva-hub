## 2. How this hits ~€70 AOV
Most carts are small (a ball tube + grips ≈ €20). The blended ~€70 AOV depends on:
- **Bundles**: the €99.99 Starter Kit and €34.99 Match-Ready bundle pull the average up.
- **Cross-sell**: "add balls + grip" at cart/checkout; free-shipping threshold set ~€60–75 to nudge a second item.
- **Accessories attach**: dampeners/towel/bag as one-click add-ons.
- **Private-label mix**: as own-brand grips/bags/rackets grow, COGS drops and margin rises (moves you from the
  model's Conservative toward Realistic/Optimistic columns).

> Validate the real blended AOV in the first 50–100 orders and feed it back into `financial-model.xlsx`.

- **Shop by level:** Beginner · Improver · Advanced (powers the "find your racket" quiz).
- **Shop by type:** Rackets · Balls · Grips & Tape · Bags · Accessories · Apparel · Bundles.
- **Shop by use:** Indoor balls vs Outdoor balls; Control vs Power rackets.
- **Featured:** Starter Kit, Replenish Pack (subscription), Bestsellers, New In.

---

## 4. Pricing principles
- **Charge for fast EU shipping in the price**, not by undercutting margin (see model: ~38% COGS realistic).
- **Anchor** rackets/bundles next to single items so bundles look like clear value.
- **Subscribe & Save** ~10–15% off the Replenish Pack to convert repeat buyers into MRR.
- **Free shipping threshold** above single-item AOV (~€60–75) to drive basket size.
- **Don't discount the brand to death** — protect margin; use bundles/value, not constant % off.

## 5. Phasing (what to list when)
- **Launch (M1–3):** consumables + accessories + Essentials/Match-Ready bundles + a beginner racket (resale) for the Starter Kit. Apparel via POD.
- **Validate (M3–4):** introduce first **private-label** grips/bags (highest margin, lowest MOQ).
- **Scale (M5+):** launch **PULSO private-label rackets** (Tempo, then Pulse) once the audience + cash support MOQ.
