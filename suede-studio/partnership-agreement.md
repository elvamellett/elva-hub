# The Suede Studio — Partnership Agreement (One-Pager)

*A plain-English agreement between two friends who want to still be friends in five years. Fill in, read aloud together, sign two copies. This is a template, **not legal advice** — for the €20 it costs to be sure, have a solicitor glance over it before or shortly after signing, and revisit it if the business incorporates.*

---

**This agreement is between:**

**Elva Mellett** ("Elva") and **______** ("Laura"), together trading as **The Suede Studio** (business name registered / to be registered with the CRO), made on **______ 2026**.

## 1. what we're building

An ecommerce and content studio serving small businesses on Shopify. We are equal partners in it.

## 2. time — the 6-hour rule

- Each of us commits **6 hours a week**: the fixed 2-hour joint session (currently ______ evenings) plus 4 flexible solo hours.
- Hours go in the **shared time ledger**, reviewed at the joint session. Drift within ±4 hours over a month is normal life; beyond that we rebalance with tasks, not guilt.
- Either of us can flag a **low-capacity week** (exams, retail peaks, life) ideally a week ahead. The other covers client-facing deadlines only, and the covered hours are repaid within 4 weeks. Both of us flagged at once = no new commitments, protect existing deadlines only.
- We never take on work with a deadline inside a known exam window or the last two weeks before Christmas.

## 3. roles

- **Elva** owns ecommerce (setup, audits, Shopify management), AI & automation, and studio finance admin.
- **Laura** owns content, brand, client operations support, and community/DMs.
- Each client has one **account owner** (alternating) who handles all comms for that client.
- Either of us can veto taking on a client; neither of us can commit the studio solo to work over €______ without the other's OK.

## 4. money

- **Profit is split 50/50**, matching the equal-time commitment. Reviewed every quarter against the ledger — if the time model changes, the split conversation happens then, in the open.
- Money lives in the **studio bank account**. Expenses are reimbursed first; we keep a float of €______; distributions happen quarterly by agreement.
- Neither of us takes money out, signs contracts, or takes on debt for the studio without the other's agreement in writing (a text counts).
- Each of us handles our own income tax on our share (sole trader / partnership self-assessment).

## 5. the brand & the work

- The Suede Studio name, brand, website, templates and processes belong to **the partnership 50/50**, regardless of who made what.
- Client relationships belong to the studio, not to either of us personally.
- We don't take freelance work that competes directly with the studio without telling each other first.

## 6. if one of us wants out

- Either of us can leave with **one month's written notice**. We finish or hand over existing client commitments first — clients never pay for our changes.
- The leaver's share of money earned to date is paid out; the remaining partner may keep the name and brand by agreeing a fair buyout of the other's half (if we can't agree a number, we split the cost of one independent valuation and accept it).
- If we both want to stop: finish the client work, pay what's owed, split what's left 50/50, archive the brand together.

## 7. if we disagree

- Small stuff: whoever owns the domain decides (see §3).
- Big stuff we can't resolve in two weeks: we bring in one person we both trust to mediate before anything escalates. The friendship outranks the business — that's a term of this agreement, not a sentiment.

## 8. reviewing this

We re-read this one-pager every **quarter** at a joint session (same meeting as the money review). Changes only count when written on both copies and initialled.
