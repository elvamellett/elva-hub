06 — faq

Fair Questions

why shopify only?

because depth beats breadth. we know shopify inside out — every app, every setting, every shortcut — and our automations are built for it. if you're on another platform, moving you onto shopify can be part of the work; staying put means we're probably not your studio, and we'll say so honestly.

what does "ai in our operations" actually mean?

practical things, not buzzwords: product descriptions drafted in your voice, customer-service replies waiting for your approval, a weekly plain-english sales summary, reminders that never slip. you approve everything customer-facing, and you decide what's off-limits — we ask before we build.

why don't you show prices?

because a five-product candle shop and a 400-product boutique aren't the same job, and pretending they are is how you end up overpaying. we scope from your questionnaire and the free call, and you get a fixed price in writing within 3 days — no surprises after that.

what does "taking over" mean — do we lose control?

the opposite. it stays your shop, your brand, your final say — we do the running: the updating, the posting, the replying, the optimising. you get a monthly call, a weekly one-line update, and a report that tells you what we did and what it moved.

will you need our passwords?

never your actual login. shopify has proper collaborator access built in, and we remove our access the day an engagement ends. your shop stays yours.

how long do things take?

silver is delivered within 1–2 weeks. gold runs 3–4 weeks. platinum starts on the 1st of the month after your proposal is signed. we'll always give you an honest start date — if we're at capacity, we'll tell you rather than let your project queue silently.
