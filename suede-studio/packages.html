02 — the transformation

The Capsule

the project. a 3–4 week rebuild that takes your ecommerce from "set up in a panic" to "done properly" — store, brand, content system and your first automations.

What's included

  • shopify, done right — full store setup, refresh, or migration onto shopify: theme, product pages, collections, checkout, shipping, email capture, launch checklist
  • brand & content system — starter kit (wordmark direction, colours, fonts, tone of voice), content pillars, and your first month's calendar with six posts made
  • your first automations — product-description workflow, customer-service draft replies and a weekly sales summary, installed with human-approval steps
  • the handover — a written doc, a one-hour training call, and 30 days of email support
timeline

3–4 weeks from kickoff, with a short update from us every week. we'll always quote an honest start date.

best for

businesses that need the big fix once — and the natural step before handing us the keys with the atelier.

The Atelier

the retainer. you hand us the keys, and your ecommerce simply… runs. the store managed, the content made, the busywork automated — month after month.

What's included, monthly

  • store management — products, collections, promotions, apps, and one improvement sprint every month
  • content, made — 8–12 pieces a month: reels, carousels and story templates, planned, written and scheduled
  • ai operations, maintained — your automations monitored and tuned, drafts flowing every morning, new busywork automated as it appears
  • the rhythm — a 30-minute planning call each month, a one-line update each week, and a one-page report: what we did, what it moved, what's next
commitment

3-month minimum, then monthly. starts on the 1st of the month after your proposal is signed.

best for

owners who are done being their own ecommerce department. we keep atelier places deliberately limited so every client gets a studio, not a queue.

pricing

a flat monthly rate, quoted after your discovery call and fixed in writing.
