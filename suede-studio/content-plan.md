# The Suede Studio — Content Plan

*Our own marketing. Instagram-first, TikTok second. Built around one 2-hour weekly batch session, because we both have day jobs.*

*The client-facing version of this pillar method lives in `client-strategy-blueprint.md` §3 — pick 3–4 tailored pillars per client there.*

---

## 1. Channels & why

| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Primary. Where Irish small-business owners already are; the grid *is* our portfolio. | 3 feed posts/week + stories 2–3 days/week |
| **TikTok** | Secondary. Reach + the build-in-public story plays best here. Reels cross-post both ways. | 1–2/week (often the same video as an IG reel) |
| **Everything else** | Not yet. LinkedIn, newsletter, Pinterest etc. only when a client pipeline problem demands it. Two people, six hours a week — focus wins. | — |

The studio's own accounts must *demonstrate* the service: if we can't keep our own grid consistent and good, why would anyone pay us to do theirs? The brand pack's grid system (see `brand.html`) is the visual law for every post. And because "AI-powered" is the positioning, our own content should visibly *use* AI well — show the drafts, show the human edit, show the time saved.

## 2. The five pillars

Rotate through these; no week should need more than one filming/shooting effort.

### 🛒 pillar 1 — *shop talk*
**Goal:** prove ecommerce + AI expertise → drives Swatch audits. **Owner: Elva.**
**Formats:** carousels, screen-recorded TikToks, quick talking-head reels.
- "5 things killing your Shopify checkout (fix them tonight)" — carousel
- Screen-record: rewriting one real (anonymised) product description live
- "What our audit actually finds" — carousel walking through a sample roadmap
- "Your shipping settings are scaring people away" — 60-sec reel
- "The one email flow every small shop needs before Christmas" — carousel
- **AI angle (our differentiator — at least one of these a fortnight):**
  - Screen-record: "watch AI draft 10 product descriptions in your brand voice — then watch us fix the two it got wrong" (honesty = trust)
  - "3 things you should let AI do in your shop, and 3 things you never should" — carousel
  - "The weekly sales report that writes itself" — before (owner at 11pm with a spreadsheet) / after reel

### 🧠 pillar 2 — *make it make sense*
**Goal:** prove content-strategy expertise → drives strategy consults. **Owner: Laura.**
**Formats:** carousels, caption-heavy single posts.
- "You don't need to post every day. Here's the maths." — carousel
- "Content pillars, explained with a café" — carousel using persona-style example
- "What to post when you have nothing to post" — saveable list post
- "Stop posting your product. Start posting your customer." — single + strong caption
- "How we plan a month of content in 2 hours" — behind-the-scenes reel (doubles as pillar 3)

### 🏗️ pillar 3 — *behind the studio*
**Goal:** the story people follow — two friends building a studio around full-time jobs and college. Highest TikTok potential. **Owner: both, filmed at the joint session.**
**Formats:** raw reels/TikToks, story sequences.
- "We started a studio with 6 hours a week. Week 1:" — running TikTok series
- Sunday batch session timelapse — recurring, zero extra effort
- "Things we argued about this week (and how we split the work)" — honest reel
- "What launching cost us so far: €—" — transparency post
- Exam-season episode: "what happens to the business when one of us disappears for two weeks"

### ✨ pillar 4 — *the glow-up*
**Goal:** visual proof of work → the portfolio pillar, converts best. **Owner: whoever did the work.**
**Formats:** before/after carousels, makeover reels.
- Mock makeover: pick a rough (fictional or consenting) shop page, redesign it, show before/after
- "We audited our own Instagram like a client's" — meta and honest
- Real client work (with permission) as it lands — every finished job produces one glow-up post
- "One product photo, three ways" — phone photography demo
- Grid before/after: screenshot of a messy grid vs. the same brand on our grid system

### 🍀 pillar 5 — *irish small biz love*
**Goal:** community + lead generation disguised as generosity. **Owner: alternates.**
**Formats:** spotlight posts, story shares, comment engagement.
- "3 Irish small shops doing Instagram right (and what to steal from them)" — tags = reach
- Market-day story takeover: walk a local market, share favourite stalls
- "Free idea for a business we love" — one genuinely useful unsolicited tip, tagged
- Monthly "small biz Saturday" story roundup of follower businesses
- Comment routine (see §4) counts as this pillar's daily form

## 3. Cadence — the honest version

**Standard week** (produced in the one 2-hour joint session, scheduled ahead):
- **Mon:** feed post (value pillar — shop talk or make it make sense)
- **Wed:** feed post (glow-up or irish small biz love)
- **Fri:** feed post/reel (behind the studio — filmed that week's session)
- Stories on 2–3 days (batch-session BTS, reshares, polls)
- 1–2 TikToks = the week's reel(s) cross-posted

**Minimum viable week** (either partner has flagged low capacity — see business plan §5):
- 1 pre-made feed post (pull from the evergreen folder)
- Stories on 2 days (low effort: reshares, a poll, a work-in-progress shot)
- Zero guilt. Consistency over intensity; a quiet week on this plan is still a consistent week.

**The batch session rule:** nothing is made outside the 2-hour session except stories. If a week's plan can't be made in 2 hours, the plan shrinks — not the evening. Keep an **evergreen folder** of 4–6 finished, undated posts as the shock absorber; refill it whenever a session runs light.

## 4. Engagement routine

15 minutes a day, alternating days (E: Mon/Wed/Fri · L: Tue/Thu/Sat, Sunday off):
- Reply to every comment and DM (leads live here — pipeline sheet anything warm)
- Leave 5 genuine comments on Irish small-business accounts (not "😍 love this!" — actual sentences; this is pillar 5 in daily form)
- Follow 3–5 accounts that match the client personas

## 7. What we measure (monthly, 10 minutes at a joint session)

Only numbers that change decisions:
1. **DMs/enquiries received** — the number that matters most
2. Follower growth (direction, not obsession)
3. Top 3 posts by saves + shares (saves ≈ value, shares ≈ reach) → make more of whatever won
4. Which pillar drove the enquiries → weight the next month's rotation toward it

Skip likes. Skip daily checking. If a month produces zero enquiries, the fix is usually more pillar 4 (proof) and pillar 5 (community), not more posting.
