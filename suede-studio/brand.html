01 — wordmark

Two lockups. No logo needed.

the wordmark is typography, not an image — it can never pixelate, and it always matches the brand font.

The Suede Studio

inline — nav, footer, email signatures

The Suede
Studio

stacked — hero, covers, title slides

S

monogram — favicon, profile pictures, watermark

  • clear space: leave at least the height of the “S” empty around every side of the wordmark.
  • minimum size: if the inline lockup drops below ~120px wide, use the monogram instead.
  • always caps, always tracked: letter-spacing 0.18em. never title case, never condensed manually.
  • colours: brown on cream, or cream on brown with the ice rule. nothing else.
  • never: stretch it, add a drop shadow, set it in another font, or place it over a busy photo.

03 — typography

Caps speak. Lowercase explains.

one family, two behaviours: headings are all caps and bold; body copy is lowercase, regular and smaller. the contrast between the two is the typographic style.

Pragmatica & the fallback

Pragmatica is the brand typeface. it’s a commercial font (licensed via paratype/myfonts), so it isn’t bundled here. until the licence is bought, everything renders in Archivo — a free google-fonts grotesque chosen because it shares pragmatica’s neutral, slightly condensed feel with strong bold caps.

to switch to real pragmatica: buy the webfont (book + bold weights are enough), drop the .woff2 files beside this html file, and uncomment the @font-face block at the top of the file. because 'Pragmatica' is already first in the font stack, the whole document switches automatically.

04 — tone of voice

Warm. Plain-spoken. Quietly expert.

we write like a knowledgeable friend, not an agency. lowercase in captions and body copy is part of the voice — relaxed, never sloppy.

Warm

“your shop deserves better product photos — here’s how to take them on your phone.”

“businesses must optimise visual assets to maximise conversion.”

Plain-spoken

“your checkout is asking people for too much. cut two fields, sell more.”

“we leverage synergistic funnel optimisation strategies.”

Quietly expert

“we manage ecommerce for a living. this is what actually works.”

“🚀🚀 THE #1 SECRET SHOPIFY HACK THEY DON’T WANT YOU TO KNOW 🚀🚀”

  • captions and body copy: lowercase, first person plural (“we”), short sentences.
  • headings and calls to action: caps, confident, no exclamation marks needed.
  • the punch line device: short caps statements ending in full stops — “the store. handled.” “the busywork. automated.” — used in tickers, tiles and story covers.
  • emoji: sparing — one, sometimes, where it earns its place. never strings of them.
  • never promise “growth hacks”, “secrets” or overnight results. we sell done-properly.
05 — do / don’t

The fast rules

Caps heading lowercase body underneath, plenty of space.
do caps headings + lowercase body on cream.
Ice on brown accent text always sits on the dark brown.
do use ice text on brown for contrast moments.
Tinted section ice-light bands break up long cream pages.
do use ice-light as a section background tint.
Ice on cream can you even read this? exactly.
don’t put ice text on cream — it fails contrast.
Title Case Heading Here headings are never title case or sentence case.
don’t use title-case headings. caps or nothing.
Third colour?? no pinks, no gradients, no “just this once”.
don’t introduce extra colours or gradients.
