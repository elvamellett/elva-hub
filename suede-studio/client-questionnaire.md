# The Suede Studio — Client Questionnaire

*The intake form every prospective client fills out before their discovery call. Build it as a free Google Form (instructions at the bottom), link it from the website, and read every answer before the call so the call starts at "we've read everything — let's talk about what matters."*

---

## Form intro text

> **THE SUEDE STUDIO — LET'S GET TO KNOW YOUR BUSINESS**
>
> this takes about 10 minutes, and it's the most useful 10 minutes of the whole process — the more honest you are here, the better your proposal will be. nothing you write is shared outside the studio, and there are no wrong answers.
>
> — elva & laura

---

## Section 1 — The basics

1. Your name *(short answer, required)*
2. Business name *(short answer, required)*
3. Your role in the business *(short answer — e.g. owner, manager)*
4. Email address *(short answer, required)*
5. Phone / WhatsApp *(short answer)*
6. Website URL *(short answer)*
7. Instagram / TikTok handles *(short answer)*
8. Where is the business based? *(short answer — town/county)*
9. How did you hear about us? *(multiple choice: Instagram · TikTok · referral from a friend/business · met you in person · other)*

## Section 2 — Your business

10. What do you sell, in a sentence or two? *(paragraph, required)*
11. How long has the business been trading? *(multiple choice: under 1 year · 1–3 years · 3–10 years · 10+ years)*
12. How many people work in the business, including you? *(multiple choice: just me · 2–5 · 6–10 · more than 10)*
13. Do you have a physical shop or premises? *(multiple choice: yes, a shop · yes, a studio/workshop · markets/pop-ups only · fully online)*
14. Roughly how do your sales split today? *(multiple choice: mostly in person · mostly online · about half and half · we barely sell online yet)*
15. What made you reach out to us **now**? What's the final straw? *(paragraph, required — this answer is usually the whole brief)*

## Section 3 — Your Shopify store

*Section description: we work exclusively with Shopify — it's where we're deepest. if you're not on it yet, don't worry: moving you onto it can be part of the work.*

16. Are you currently on Shopify? *(multiple choice, required: yes · no, but open to moving · no, and I want to stay on my current platform*†*)*
    † *Form logic: if they pick the third option, show a polite message that we're Shopify-only and may not be the right fit — saves everyone a call.*
17. If yes — how long has the store been live? *(multiple choice: under 6 months · 6 months–2 years · 2+ years · not live yet)*
18. Who built it? *(multiple choice: I did · a friend/family member · a freelancer/agency · previous staff · honestly, it's complicated)*
19. Roughly how many orders a month does it do? *(multiple choice: 0–10 · 10–50 · 50–200 · 200+ · prefer to discuss on the call)*
20. Roughly how many products do you have listed? *(multiple choice: under 20 · 20–100 · 100–400 · 400+)*
21. What apps/integrations are you using, if you know them? *(paragraph — e.g. Klaviyo, Judge.me, Planet, loyalty apps)*
22. What frustrates you most about your store right now? *(paragraph, required)*
23. Is there a store (any brand, any size) whose online shop you'd love yours to feel like? *(short answer)*

## Section 4 — Products & fulfilment

24. Who packs and ships orders, and how long does it take each week? *(paragraph)*
25. What's your returns/exchanges situation — smooth, painful, or nonexistent? *(short answer)*
26. Do your products change with seasons/collections, or stay mostly the same year round? *(multiple choice: seasonal drops · steady core range · both)*
27. Where do product photos come from today? *(multiple choice: my phone · a photographer · suppliers' images · a mix · what product photos)*

## Section 5 — Content & socials

28. Which channels is the business on? *(checkboxes: Instagram · TikTok · Facebook · email newsletter · Pinterest · LinkedIn · none really)*
29. Who posts, and how often — honestly? *(paragraph, required)*
30. What's the hardest part of content for you? *(checkboxes: finding time · knowing what to post · filming/photographing · writing captions · being on camera · staying consistent · all of it)*
31. Do you have brand assets — logo, colours, fonts, a style guide? *(multiple choice: yes, proper ones · sort of — a logo and vibes · no, and it shows · no, but I'd like some)*
32. Describe your brand in three words — how you'd *like* people to see it. *(short answer)*
33. Any accounts (competitors or otherwise) whose content you admire? *(short answer)*

## Section 6 — Operations & busywork

34. List the top 3 tasks that eat your week — the repetitive stuff you dread. *(paragraph, required — this feeds the automation map)*
35. What tools run the back office today? *(checkboxes: email · spreadsheets · paper/notebooks · accounting software · inventory software · a drawer of receipts · other)*
36. If you could never do one task again, which would it be? *(short answer)*
37. How do customer questions reach you, and how long do they wait for an answer? *(paragraph — e.g. Instagram DMs, email, phone; same day / days / oops)*

## Section 7 — AI & automation

*Section description: part of what makes us different — we build AI into your everyday operations: drafting product descriptions, customer-service replies, weekly sales summaries, and more. always with you in control.*

38. Are you using any AI tools in the business today? *(multiple choice: yes, regularly · dabbled a bit · no, but curious · no, and sceptical)*
39. How would you feel about AI **drafting** things for you (you'd still approve before anything goes out)? *(linear scale 1–5: 1 = not comfortable at all → 5 = sign me up)*
40. Is there anything you would **never** want automated? *(paragraph — e.g. "replies to complaints", "anything customers see")*

## Section 8 — Goals, budget & fit

41. If we work together and it goes brilliantly — what does the business look like in 6 months? *(paragraph, required)*
42. Which sounds most like what you need? *(multiple choice: a one-off expert once-over and a plan [THE SWATCH] · a proper rebuild, done for me [THE CAPSULE] · take it all off my plate, month to month [THE ATELIER] · honestly, tell me what I need)*
43. What monthly or project budget feels comfortable? *(multiple choice: under €500 · €500–€1,000 · €1,000–€2,500 · €2,500+ · prefer to discuss on the call)*
44. When would you want to start? *(multiple choice: yesterday · within a month · within 3 months · just exploring for now)*
45. Anything else we should know — about the business, the timing, or you? *(paragraph)*

## Form closing text

> **that's it — thank you.**
> we read every answer properly (both of us). you'll hear from us within one business day to set up your free 20-minute discovery call. talk soon.
> — the suede studio · hello@thesuedestudio.com

## Setting it up in Google Forms (10 minutes)

1. forms.google.com → blank form → title: **The Suede Studio — Client Questionnaire**; paste the intro text as the description.
2. Add each section above with "Add section" (the ⧉ icon) so the form shows progress and doesn't feel long — 8 short sections beat one wall of questions.
3. Question types are marked inline: *(short answer)*, *(paragraph)*, *(multiple choice)*, *(checkboxes)*, *(linear scale)*. Mark the ones tagged **required**.
4. Q16 Shopify logic: use "Go to section based on answer" — the "want to stay on my current platform" option routes to a short closing section: *"we work exclusively with shopify, so we may not be the right fit — but if you ever consider moving, we'd love to talk."*
5. Settings → Responses → link a spreadsheet. That spreadsheet is the intake half of the pipeline sheet (business plan §6).
6. Settings → turn ON "Collect email addresses"; leave "Limit to 1 response" OFF (no Google login hurdle for busy owners).
7. Send → link icon → shorten URL → this is the link the website's "start with the questionnaire" buttons point to (swap the placeholder marked `YOUR-GOOGLE-FORM-LINK` in `index.html` and `packages.html`).
8. Test it yourselves once end-to-end before it goes live — fill it in as a fake café and read what lands in the sheet.
