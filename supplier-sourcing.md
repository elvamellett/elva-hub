# Supplier & Sourcing Guide (Padel)

How to source padel inventory across the three main routes, matched to the **accessory/consumable-led →
private-label** strategy. The right answer is usually a **blend** that shifts over time, not a single supplier.

> Supplier names below are examples surfaced in 2026 research — **vet them yourself**; availability,
> pricing, and reliability change. Always order samples before selling.

---

## 1. The three routes at a glance

| Factor | **EU / UK suppliers & distributors** | **China sourcing agent** | **Print-on-Demand (POD)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Margin** | Lower (you buy near-retail wholesale) | **Highest** (factory-direct, esp. private-label) | Low–medium (per-unit print cost) |
| **Shipping time to EU customer** | **Fastest (2–7 days)** | Slow on dropship (10–20+ days); fast once stocked in EU | Medium (3–10 days, printer-dependent) |
| **MOQ / upfront cash** | Low (buy as you go) | **High** for private-label (50–500+ units) | **None** (made to order) |
| **Branding control** | Limited (resell others' brands) | **Full** (your logo, packaging, specs) | Logo/design on apparel only |
| **Scalability** | Good for accessories; margin caps profit | **Best long-term** (own brand, volume pricing) | Caps out; print margins stay thin |
| **Best used for** | Fast-shipping branded **balls & accessories** at launch | **Private-label** grips, bags, dampeners, then rackets | **Apparel/merch** (tees, hoodies, towels) |
| **Main risk** | Thin margins; you're a reseller | QC, lead time, MOQ cash lock-up, communication | Thin margins, generic feel, printer reliability |

**Recommended sequencing for your plan:**
1. **Launch (Month 1–3):** EU distributor for branded balls/accessories (fast shipping builds trust & reviews)
   + POD for branded apparel (zero inventory risk).
2. **Validate (Month 3–4):** once an offer proves out, place a **small private-label order** via a China agent
   for the highest-margin, lowest-MOQ items first — **overgrips and bags**.
3. **Scale (Month 5+):** expand private-label range; introduce a **private-label hero racket** only once the
   audience and cash support the MOQ.

## 2. Route detail

### A) EU / UK suppliers & distributors
**Why start here:** fast 2–7 day delivery is a conversion and trust advantage, and you avoid customs/long
lead times while you're still learning. Ideal for **branded padel balls** (a known consumable) and
ready-made accessories.

- **Pros:** speed, reliability, easy returns, no import hassle, buy small.
- **Cons:** you're reselling others' brands at wholesale → margins are thinner; less differentiation.
- **Examples to vet:** general EU dropship distributors (VidaXL-type marketplaces for some accessories),
  wellness/sports distributors (e.g. AWGifts-type), multi-warehouse platforms (e.g. EPROLO with EU stock).
  For *padel-specific* stock, also approach **national padel/tennis wholesale distributors** directly —
  many offer trade accounts.
- **Tip:** ask every distributor for a **trade/wholesale account** (better than dropship-marketplace pricing).

### B) China sourcing agent (the margin & brand engine)
**Why it matters:** this is where real margin and a defensible private-label brand come from. A good agent
sources from factories, handles QC, consolidates, and can warehouse stock in the EU for fast fulfilment.

- **Pros:** factory-direct cost, full branding/packaging control, custom specs, volume discounts.
- **Cons:** MOQs lock up cash; lead times (production + freight) of weeks; QC and communication risk;
  you must order samples and inspect.
- **Sequencing by MOQ/risk:** start with **overgrips → dampeners → wristbands → bags** (low MOQ, low unit cost,
  high margin) before committing to **rackets** (higher MOQ, higher unit cost, quality-sensitive on feel).
- **Logistics:** for dropship-speed, have the agent **bulk-ship to an EU 3PL/warehouse**, then fulfil locally
  (2–5 day delivery) instead of single-parcel-from-China (10–20+ days).
- **Examples to vet:** dedicated sourcing agents and platforms (CJdropshipping for early dropship + EU
  warehousing; independent agents for true private-label). Get multiple quotes.

### C) Print-on-Demand (POD)
**Why include it:** zero inventory risk for **branded apparel/merch** — a natural padel add-on (tees,
hoodies, caps, microfibre towels, bag tags) that builds community identity and lifts AOV.

- **Pros:** no upfront stock, no MOQ, instant new designs, brand-building.
- **Cons:** thin per-unit margin, generic base products, printer-dependent quality/speed; **order samples**.
- **Use it for:** community/identity merch and testing apparel designs — **not** for your core hero economics.
- **Examples to vet:** Printful/Printify-type providers with **EU printing** for faster delivery.

## 3. Margin reality check (illustrative — validate with real quotes)

| Item | Route | Typical cost | Typical retail | Gross margin | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Padel balls (3-can) | EU distributor | €5–8 | €10–15 | ~40–50% | Consumable / repeat hook |
| Overgrips (pack) | China private-label | €1–2 | €7–10 | **~75–85%** | High-margin consumable |
| Vibration dampener | China private-label | €0.50–1.50 | €6–10 | **~80%+** | Impulse add-on |
| Racket bag | China private-label | €8–15 | €35–55 | **~65–75%** | AOV builder |
| Apparel (tee) | POD (EU) | €10–14 | €25–35 | ~45–55% | Identity / community |
| Hero racket | China private-label | €25–45 | €80–130 | ~55–65% | Brand moat (later) |

The pattern: **private-label consumables/accessories carry the margin; branded resale carries the trust;
POD carries the identity.** Blend accordingly.

## 4. Supplier / agent vetting question bank

Ask **before** committing money. Treat vague or evasive answers as a red flag.

### Product & quality
- Can you send **samples** before any bulk order? (Non-negotiable — always sample.)
- What are the exact **materials/specs** (ball pressure/felt; grip thickness/tack; racket core EVA/carbon)?
- Do you have **CE / EN safety compliance** docs where relevant? Test reports?
- What's your **defect/QC rate**, and what's your QC process before shipping?
- Can you do **custom branding/packaging** (logo, colourway, box)? At what MOQ?

### Pricing & terms
- **Unit price at MOQ 50 / 100 / 250 / 500** (get the volume curve)?
- What's the **MOQ** per SKU, and can you **mix SKUs** to reach it?
- **Payment terms** — deposit %, balance timing, accepted methods, escrow (e.g. Trade Assurance)?
- Any **setup/mould/tooling fees** for private-label?

### Logistics & lead time
- **Production lead time** + **shipping time** to the EU (air vs sea)?
- Can you ship to / do you operate an **EU warehouse/3PL** for fast local fulfilment?
- Who handles **customs/duties/VAT** — and what's the landed cost?
- Can you support **blind dropshipping** (no supplier branding in the parcel) early on?

### Reliability & relationship
- How long in business; can you share **references / other brands** you supply?
- Typical **reorder lead time** once we're set up?
- How do you handle **damaged/lost shipments and returns**?
- Who is my **direct contact**, time zone, and response-time expectation?
- **Capacity:** can you handle 10×/50× our volume if we scale?

### Risk & exclusivity
- Any **exclusivity** possible on a custom design/colourway?
- What happens to **my designs/moulds** if we stop working together?
- Are there **IP/patent issues** with any product you'd sell me? (Especially racket tech.)

---

## 5. Sourcing red flags
- Refuses to provide samples, or pressures you to skip sampling.
- No verifiable history/references; brand-new entity only.
- Prices that seem too good (often QC or counterfeit risk).
- Evasive on compliance/IP, lead times, or who handles customs.
- Pushes a huge first MOQ before you've validated demand.
- Poor/slow communication during *courtship* (it only gets worse later).

---

## 6. Cash & MOQ discipline (protect your runway)
- Validate with **EU resale + POD (low/no MOQ)** *before* spending on private-label inventory.
- First private-label buy: smallest viable MOQ on the **highest-margin, lowest-cost** items (grips/bags).
- Keep a **cash reserve** — inventory + scaling ad spend can both demand cash *before* profit arrives.
- Reorder *ahead* of stockouts: factor production + freight lead time into your reorder point.
